Why we built it

A gas leak gives no second chance

LPG is heavier than air, collects near the floor and only needs a spark – a light switch, a phone charger – to ignite. Most kitchen leaks happen when nobody is home or everybody is asleep. A simple buzzer can't reach you at the office. GLD does three things at once:

1. Detect

The MQ-2 sensor measures LPG, propane and smoke twice every second.

2. Act automatically

Exhaust fan ON, servo closes the gas valve, the light circuit is switched OFF to avoid sparks.

3. Alert anywhere

Push notification to every family phone, a siren in the app, live gas level and history.

Step by step

What happens when gas leaks

From the first gas molecule to the notification on your phone – usually in under two seconds. Press “Gas leak!” in the simulator above to see these steps light up.

1

Sense

ESP32 reads the MQ-2 every 0.5 s. First 60 s = sensor warm-up, ignored.

2

Decide

Value ≥ 400 → leak. Back to normal only below 200, so it never flickers.

3

Act locally

Relay → fan ON, relay → light OFF, servo → valve 180° closed. No internet needed.

4

Report

ESP32 writes the status and a “leak” event to Firebase Realtime Database.

5

Alert

ESP32 sends one push via Firebase Cloud Messaging to topic gas_alert.

6

Show

Every signed-in phone: notification, red dashboard, 5 s siren, emergency numbers.

Hardware

Components and wiring

Everything runs on one ESP32 board. A 5 V 2 A adapter powers the sensor, servo, fan and light; all grounds are connected together.

Cloud & data

What is stored and how the app uses it

The ESP32 is the only one allowed to write. The app only reads. Times are filled in by Firebase's own clock.

Firebase Realtime Database

devices
└─ gas01
   ├─ state        // updated every 60 s + on every change
   │   ├─ gas:       false
   │   ├─ value:     128      // MQ-2 reading
   │   ├─ threshold: 400      // alarm level

Design decisions

Built to be reliable

Safety works without internet

Fan, valve and light are controlled by the ESP32 itself. WiFi and the cloud are only for alerts.

One alert per leak, not one per second

Alerts are sent only when the state changes. An earlier Blynk version hit its 100-alerts-per-day limit in under a minute – this design never does.

No false alarms on start-up

60-second sensor warm-up, plus two thresholds (400 on / 200 off) so a reading near the limit can't flicker.

WiFi set from the phone

No re-programming: hold BOOT, join “GasDetector-Setup”, pick your WiFi. Auto-reconnects after power cuts.

Know when you're not protected

If the detector stops reporting for 2.5 minutes, the app shows “Detector offline”. Alerts missed while offline are sent after reconnecting.

Help is one tap away

During a leak the app shows what to do and call buttons for 1906 (LPG emergency), 101 (fire) and 112.

Many users, one detector

Every signed-in phone gets the alert (topic messaging). Signing out stops alerts on that phone.

Free to run

Firebase free (Spark) plan: Realtime Database and Cloud Messaging cost nothing at this scale.
